3D Cover

Turns flat book covers into 3D hardcover books, with spine, page edges and shadow.

Author: Dunhill
Inspired by the "Create Hardcover" plugin by sornay.

Features:
- Realistic 3D hardcover with spine, page edges and drop shadow
- Background filled with the cover grid color (calibre stores covers as JPEG, without transparency)
- Selectable output size: Normal, Large, Extra large or Original
- RTL support for Arabic, Hebrew, Persian books
- Batch processing with progress bars
- Save and restore original covers
- Keyboard navigation
