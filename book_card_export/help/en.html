Book Card Export — Help

Creates a book card (cover, details and synopsis, like the one calibre shows) to save it as an image or share it in Telegram: the cover as a photo and the details and synopsis as its caption.

1. Opening the card

  • Select a book in the list and click the Book card button in the toolbar.
  • With one book selected, Previous and Next go through all the books in the list (the whole library, or the search results if a search is active).
  • With several books selected, you go through those only, and a button to export them all appears.
  • Next to the buttons you see where you are, for example 9 of 319 books in the library.

2. The card window

On the left, the preview of the card as it will be saved:

  • Copy card image: copies the whole card as an image, to paste it anywhere (Ctrl+V).
  • Save image...: saves it as PNG or JPG (chosen in the configuration).
  • Export the N selected as images...: saves one image per book in the folder you choose. Only shown when the window was opened with several books selected.

On the right, the text for Telegram:

  • It can be edited before copying it. Restore text discards your changes.
  • **Two asterisks** become bold and __two underscores__ become italic when pasted in Telegram.
  • The counter shows the number of characters: green if it fits in a photo caption, red if not.
  • I have Telegram Premium: a photo caption allows 1024 characters, or 4096 with Premium.

3. Sharing in Telegram, step by step

If the text fits (green counter):

  1. Click 1. Copy cover.
  2. In Telegram, paste it with Ctrl+V: the window to send the photo opens.
  3. Click 2. Copy text and paste it in the caption field of that window. Send.

If it does not fit (red counter), the text is split in two parts, between paragraphs:

  1. 1. Copy cover and paste it in Telegram.
  2. 2. Copy part 1 and paste it in the photo caption. It ends with “⬇️ The synopsis continues in the next message”. Send.
  3. 3. Copy part 2 and send it as a new message.

You can also send the whole card image (Copy card image button) instead of the cover.

4. Configuration

From the arrow of the button → Configure..., or with the Configure button of the window.

General tab

  • Background: According to the theme (white or dark), Custom color (with a color picker) or Transparent. The Quick colors button has colors that match Telegram (dark and light), so the card blends into the chat.
  • Theme: the text color. Light (dark text) or Dark (light text). When you choose a background color it is set automatically so the text can be read, and you can change it.
  • Image format: PNG or JPG. With a transparent background PNG is always used.
  • Add “Exported from calibre” at the bottom.
  • I have Telegram Premium and Add the tags as hashtags at the end of the text (#Thriller #Novel), useful to search in channels and groups.
  • Where to show the emojis: four independent boxes, for the Telegram text and the card image, in front of each field and on each genre.

Fields tab

  • By default the card shows the same fields as calibre's book details panel, in the same order.
  • If you untick that option, you choose the fields to include and drag them to change the order. Title, authors and synopsis are always included.
  • Double click a field (or Change emoji...) to choose its emoji. Title and authors are at the top.

Genres tab

  • Lists the tags of your library with the number of books of each one, and has a search box.
  • Double click a genre to choose its emoji. Remove emoji deletes it.
  • Suggest emojis assigns the usual emoji to the genres that have none (Fantasy 🐉, Science fiction 🚀, Romance 💕, Horror 👻, Crime 🕵️...). You can change them afterwards.

Style tab

  • Extra CSS for the card image. Classes: .title, .author, .label (field name), .value, .comments (synopsis), .footer.
  • Example, green italic synopsis:
    .comments { color: #007a00; font-style: italic; font-weight: bold; }

5. The emoji picker

  • It has tabs by category: books, people, numbers, ratings, places, genres, faces and symbols.
  • If one is missing, paste it in Other emoji and click Use it, or Add to my emojis to keep it in your own palette (My emojis tab; right click to remove it).
  • No emoji: the field is shown with just its name.

6. Common problems

  • Emojis look blank in the text panel. Your system has no color emoji font. They look fine when pasted in Telegram anyway. On Linux, install the font (sudo apt install fonts-noto-color-emoji) and restart calibre. In the picker, the configuration and the card image emojis are always shown, because the plugin includes its own images.
  • The transparent background turns white. Only PNG keeps transparency. Telegram always shows images over a white background, even when sent as a file: for Telegram, use one of the Quick colors. Some image viewers also paint the background white even if it is transparent.
  • A field is missing or there is one too many. Check the Fields tab, or the fields shown by calibre's book details panel (Preferences → Look & feel → Book details).
